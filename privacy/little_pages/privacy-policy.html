Little Pages: Journal — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short

Little Pages: Journal (called Minik Sayfalar: Günlük in Turkish) is a journal and memory book app. You never create an account. The pages, photos, voice recordings and notes you write are stored only on your device. We do not collect them, send them to our servers or share them with anyone. We show no ads, do not track you and do not analyze your usage.

Who is responsible

Ekin Emre Atasoy, studyosefo@gmail.com. Because the app does not send your personal data to us, we do not process the data described in this policy; it stays on your device.

What stays on your device

While you use the app you create the following, and all of it is stored only on your device:

  • Your journal pages (title, text, mood, date), tags, stickers and special days
  • Photos and voice notes
  • Profile details such as your name, your baby's name or nickname, and the due date or birth date
  • Your settings (handwriting, color, reminder time and so on)

We cannot access this data. If you delete the app from your device, this data is deleted too. That is why you can make your own backup as a ZIP file with Back up my pages in Settings. You decide where the backup file is saved (Files, iCloud Drive, email and so on); from that point on its protection depends on that service's rules.

Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when it needs them. Each one is optional and you can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Camera: to take a photo for a page.
  • Microphone: to record a voice note.
  • Face ID / passcode: to unlock the app lock. The app never receives biometric data; iOS does the verification.
  • Notifications: for reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Photos: when you pick photos, only the photos you choose are given to the app.

Purchases

Pro is bought once through Apple's in-app purchase system. We never see your payment details (card number and so on); Apple handles the transaction and Apple's privacy policy applies. The app checks with Apple's system whether a purchase is valid; no information comes to us for this.

If you enter a code for a lower price, the code is checked only on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Tracking, ads and analytics

  • We do not track you and collect no data for advertising.
  • We use no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.
  • We do not sell your data.

If we add anonymous measurement in the future to understand which features are used or to find crashes, we will announce it in this policy first and ask you clearly. Such measurement would never include page text, photos, names, dates, pregnancy status, week or location.

Children

The app is for parents and parents-to-be. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (we do not collect personal data at all).

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because we do not process your personal data, requests to access, correct or delete it are usually answered by the app on your device: you can edit or delete pages in the app, and Erase everything in Settings removes all data from your device. If you have questions about this policy, write to studyosefo@gmail.com.

Security

Your data is stored with iOS device protection. You can add extra protection with the app lock. The backup file is not encrypted; be careful who you share it with.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For a meaningful change we will also say so in the app.
