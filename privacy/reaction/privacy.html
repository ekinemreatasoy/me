Privacy Policy — Reaction

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Reaction ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.

On-device data

Your game scores, streaks, and settings are stored only on your device using Apple's local storage (SwiftData). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Deleting the app removes it.

Network

The app makes no network requests. It works fully offline.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs or trackers.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local reminders to help you keep your daily streak. These are generated entirely on your device; no data is sent to us or anyone else. You can disable notifications anytime in iOS Settings.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new date.
